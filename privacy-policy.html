Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information when you use the Randall-Penn Constructions website or contact us.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Randall-Penn Constructions (we, us or our) handles personal information collected through this website and in connection with website enquiries and consultations. We are committed to handling personal information responsibly. Where the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles apply to us, we handle personal information in accordance with those requirements.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, project suburb, project type, project details, correspondence with us, appointment information and other information you choose to provide.

When you use the website, technical and usage information may also be collected, such as your device or browser type, approximate location derived from your IP address, pages viewed, session information, referral source and interactions with the website.

3. How we collect information

We may collect information directly from you when you submit the contact form, contact us by phone or email, book a consultation, or otherwise communicate with us. We may also collect website usage information automatically through analytics technologies.

4. Why we use information

We may use personal information to respond to enquiries, arrange consultations, assess and discuss potential projects, communicate with clients and prospective clients, operate and improve the website, understand website performance, maintain security, keep business records and comply with legal obligations.

5. Google Analytics and website usage data

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to help us understand how visitors use the website. GA4 may collect information about website visits and interactions and uses first-party cookies to distinguish users and sessions. Our current Google Analytics measurement ID is G-ETXDV4F32J.

For more information about the cookies used on this website, see our Cookie Policy.

6. Calendly and appointment booking

Our Contact page includes an embedded Calendly scheduling tool. If you choose to use it, information you provide through that booking experience is processed by Calendly in accordance with its own terms and privacy practices. Calendly may also use cookies or similar technologies when the embedded booking tool is loaded or used.

7. Service providers and disclosure

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to service providers that support our website and business operations, including website hosting, analytics, scheduling, email delivery and business communications. These providers may include Vercel, Google, Calendly, Resend and our email and technology providers.

We may also disclose information to professional advisers, insurers, contractors or government authorities where reasonably necessary or required by law. We do not sell personal information.

8. Overseas processing

Some of our technology providers operate globally and may process or store information outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries in which they or their service providers operate. The locations used by a provider may change over time.

9. Storage, security and retention

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the information we hold to protect it from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain information for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business record-keeping, or as required by law, and then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

10. Access and correction

You may contact us to request access to personal information we hold about you or to ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

11. Privacy questions or complaints

If you have a privacy question, concern or complaint, contact us using the details on this page. We will consider the matter and respond within a reasonable period. If the Privacy Act applies to the matter and you are not satisfied with our response, you may also have the right to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

12. External websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies and are not controlled by us.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices, technology or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with the date it was last updated.
