1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website or service. Similar technologies, including browser local storage, can also be used to remember settings or support website functionality.

2. How this website uses cookies

Randall-Penn Constructions uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, understand website performance and support embedded third-party services.

3. Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), measurement ID G-ETXDV4F32J, to understand how visitors find and use our website. Google states that GA4 JavaScript tags use first-party cookies to distinguish users and sessions.

CookieTypical purposeDefault expiry
_gaUsed by Google Analytics to distinguish users.2 years
_ga_<container-id>Used by Google Analytics to persist session state.2 years

Cookie duration can also be affected by browser controls and browser-specific limits.

4. Calendly

The Contact page contains an embedded Calendly scheduling tool. Calendly may use cookies or similar technologies to provide its booking experience, remember settings, maintain security and measure use of its service. Cookies set by Calendly are controlled by Calendly.

5. Our cookie notice

We display a short privacy and cookie notice to make visitors aware of analytics and embedded services. When you dismiss that notice, the website stores a small preference in your browser's local storage so the notice does not need to appear on every page. Local storage is not an advertising cookie.

6. Controlling cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website or embedded-service functionality. You can also use browser privacy features and tools provided by analytics providers to limit tracking.

7. Personal information

Information collected through cookies or similar technologies may in some circumstances be personal information. For more detail about how we handle personal information and the service providers we use, see our Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when the technologies used on the website change. The current version will be published on this page with the date it was last updated.

9. Contact

If you have questions about cookies or privacy on this website, contact Randall-Penn Constructions using the details on this page.
