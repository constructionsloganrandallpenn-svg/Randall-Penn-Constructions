Services

One project.
One point of contact.

Randall-Penn Constructions is set up to handle more than the build itself. From structural work and renovations through to landscaping, site preparation, clean-up and the finishing details, the aim is to keep the project coordinated without sending you between multiple contractors.

SERVICE DETAILS TO CONFIRMThese service groups reflect the broader end-to-end offering. We’ll refine the individual inclusions once the complete service list is confirmed.
01

Construction & renovations

Residential construction and renovation work brought together around the home, the brief and the level of finish required — from larger building work through to considered upgrades.

02

Extensions & additions

Creating additional space that works with the existing home, with attention to practical integration, buildability and a finish that feels like it belongs.

03

Landscaping & outdoor works

Landscaping and outdoor improvements that carry the project beyond the building itself, helping the surrounding spaces work as part of the finished property.

04

Site preparation & clearing

Preparing and clearing work areas so the next stage can move forward efficiently, with the site considered as part of the overall project rather than a separate problem to organise.

05

Site clean-up & waste removal

Construction waste, offcuts and general site rubbish managed and removed to help keep the job organised during the works and leave the property in order at completion.

06

Carpentry & finishing works

Carpentry, detailing and finishing work that brings the final stages together, with the same attention carried through from the main build to the smaller details.

How we approach a build

Clear steps.
Fewer surprises.

Efficient Sustainable Builds is the value line behind the brand. On site, that means thinking ahead about the job, the materials and the way the work is carried through.

01 / Discuss

Understand the job

Start with the home, the goals, the constraints and the level of finish expected.

02 / Define

Clarify the scope

Work through what is included, what still needs decisions and what information is required before building.

03 / Build

Execute carefully

Keep the work practical, coordinated and focused on the quality of the finished result.

04 / Finish

Close out the details

Give the final junctions, surfaces and everyday-use details the same attention as the main build.
