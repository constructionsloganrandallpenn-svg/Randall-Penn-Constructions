Services

One project.
One point of contact.

Rather than being brought in for one isolated part of the job, Randall-Penn is set up to undertake work across the property. That means fewer moving parts to manage and a more coordinated path from the first site works through to the finishing touches.

01

Construction & renovations

Residential construction and renovation work shaped around the existing home, the brief and the level of finish required, from substantial transformations through to focused building work.

02

Extensions & additions

New space designed and built to work naturally with the existing property, including ground-floor extensions and second-storey additions.

03

Landscaping & outdoor works

Landscaping and outdoor construction that carries the project beyond the building itself, helping the home and the surrounding property work together.

04

Site preparation & clearing

Practical preparation, clearing and enabling works to get the site ready and keep the next stages of construction moving efficiently.

05

Site clean-up & waste removal

Construction waste, offcuts and general site rubbish managed as part of keeping the job organised and leaving the site in order.

06

Carpentry & finishing works

Hands-on carpentry, custom details and finishing work, including the kind of staircase, timber and interior detailing that gives a project its character.

How we work

Whole-job thinking.

Efficient Sustainable Builds is not a styling line. It is the working principle behind planning carefully, sequencing the job properly, selecting materials with consideration and keeping the finished result in view throughout the build.

01

Understand the property.

Start with what is already there, what needs to change and what the finished home needs to do better.

02

Coordinate the work.

Bring the building, site and finishing stages together so the project is not treated as disconnected pieces.

03

Build with intent.

Make practical decisions around workmanship, energy performance and material selection without losing sight of the details.
