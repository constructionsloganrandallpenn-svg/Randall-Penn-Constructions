About Randall-Penn

Craft first.
Build with purpose.

Licensed builder Logan Randall-Penn has built the business from a carpentry apprenticeship and a lifelong interest in woodworking into a residential building company focused on high-performing, considered homes.

Carpentry since 2014Randall-Penn since 2017Licensed builder since 2026
Logan Randall-Penn

Building from scratch has always been the draw.

Logan started his carpentry apprenticeship in 2014 and established Randall-Penn Constructions as a sole trader in 2017. In 2026 he became a licensed builder and established the company in its current form.

His interest in the trade began earlier. Logan's late grandfather was a woodworker, and some of his strongest early memories are of spending time with him in the workshop. That connection to making things by hand, understanding materials and building something from the ground up still sits behind the way he approaches the work today.

Randall-Penn is focused on high-performing, energy-efficient projects and a considered approach to material selection. The aim is straightforward: build well, make practical decisions and leave the home better suited to the people who live in it.

Efficient Sustainable Builds

Built with intent.

A simple principle that connects practical planning, responsible choices and the quality of the finished work.

01 / Efficient

Plan before building.

Good sequencing and clear decisions reduce unnecessary rework and keep attention on the parts of the project that matter.

02 / Sustainable

Choose with consideration.

Energy performance and material selection are considered as part of the build rather than treated as an afterthought.

03 / Built well

Respect the detail.

From structure to custom carpentry, the quality of the finished home depends on the care taken through every stage.
