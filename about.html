Logan Randall-Penn

Hands-on by nature.

Randall-Penn Constructions is led by Logan Randall-Penn, a Sydney-based carpenter. The business name dates to 2017 and the work shown publicly today remains grounded in residential renovation, carpentry and on-site building.

The brand direction is simple: Efficient Sustainable Builds. It reflects a practical approach to planning, materials, workmanship and building with the finished result in mind.

What the brand stands for

Built with intent.

Efficient Sustainable Builds gives the brand a clear direction: thoughtful planning, practical decisions and workmanship that respects the details.

01 / Efficient

Think before building.

Good sequencing and clear decisions reduce unnecessary rework and keep attention on the parts of the job that matter.

02 / Sustainable

Use resources with care.

Sustainability here is presented as a direction and mindset, not an unverified certification or performance claim.

03 / Built well

Respect the detail.

The small things — alignment, transitions, finishes and how a space is actually used — contribute to how the whole build feels.
